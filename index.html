Demo storyline

  1. Pay 200 mUSDC into the household → it splits 40/25/15/10/10 (spend / rent / mom / taxes / savings).
  2. Mom withdraws 10 mUSDC to her destination account (gasless via the relayer).
  3. The agent tries to drain savings and is blocked (not the pocket owner; FC key limited).
  4. Even the household owner cannot pull more than the savings daily cap.
